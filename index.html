Rules

Single-player test rules: The 📖 Collection Book is separate from your current-run Monster Cards. The first time you encounter a monster, it is permanently added to the browser-saved Collection Book as 👁️ Encountered. Defeating that monster marks it 🏆 Defeated and reveals its loot details. Current-run Monster Cards and equipped loot still reset when you start a new game; Collection Book discoveries persist until you manually reset the book.
